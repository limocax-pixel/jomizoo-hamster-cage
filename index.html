How the planner calculates

  1. Floor space = length × width, against the species minimum; platforms don't count.
  2. Bedding depth across the floor, plus a deeper burrowing area at the species burrowing depth.
  3. Wheel diameter, and whether bedding under the wheel + 2 cm stand + wheel fits under the lid.
  4. Bedding volume = length × width × average depth ÷ 1,000 liters, plus the compaction allowance.

Everything runs in your browser. The numbers are open data: data/species.json (v, CC BY 4.0).
